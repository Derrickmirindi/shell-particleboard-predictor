Shell-Based Particleboard Property Predictor

Linear regression predictions of TS, MOR, and IB from input properties

01 / Thickness Swelling

Thickness Swelling (TS)

TS = 0.0027 × Density + 0.2743 × WA − 2.3678
Typical ranges: Density 518–1585 kg/m³, WA 5–90%

02 / Modulus of Rupture

Modulus of Rupture (MOR)

MOR = 0.0055 × Density + 0.0021 × MOE + 1.4443
Typical ranges: Density 518–1585 kg/m³, MOE 11–4190 MPa
—
03 / Internal Bond Strength

Internal Bond Strength (IB)

IB = 0.0054 × Density + 0.0007 × MOE − 4.0890
Typical ranges: Density 518–1585 kg/m³, MOE 11–4190 MPa

This web application uses a trained Random Forest (RF) to predict the thickness swelling (TS), modulus of rupture (MOR) and internal bond strength. It provides fast estimates of the shell-based particleboards' properties with TS at R² value of 0.83, MOR R² value of 0.852, and IB R² value of 0.75. Random Forest model predictions.
